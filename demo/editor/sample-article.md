# Masks in ritual

{figure /uploads/red-mask.png | alt:"A red oval mask with dark eye holes and a dark mouth, on a dark background" | caption:"A red mask (sample image)" | credit:"draft.inator" | licence:"CC0" | align:right | width:half | wrap:yes}

A mask changes who may speak {citation bell-1992-ritual | note:"Bell 1992" | locator:94}. In the threshold between one state and the next, the wearer is neither who they were nor who they will be {citation turner-1969-ritual | note:"Turner 1969" | prefix:"see" | tags:ritual-theory}.

## What a mask does

- It hides a face, and shows another.
- It marks a time as **set apart**.
- It lets a person speak *as* someone else.
