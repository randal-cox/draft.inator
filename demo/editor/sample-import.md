Practice comes before theory (Bell, 1992, p. 94). In the threshold between one state and the next, the wearer of a mask is neither who they were nor who they will be (Turner, 1969; see also Bell, 1992).

Bell (1992) argues that ritual is a strategy, not a script. Attachment research offers a parallel: the secure base comes first (Ainsworth et al., 1978).

Masks recur across the region's festivals (Cox, 1995a), and so do river rites (Cox, 1995b). One note has no entry in the list below (Rivera, 2020), so it stays as written.
